Clean underwater clips for the Island rebuild. 1080x1920, 30fps, 1.5s each,
graded to the house look, no text and no brand marks.

  shark-clean-01.mp4   bull shark close - gills and mouth filling the frame
  shark-clean-02.mp4   the swarm - many sharks, full frame
  shark-clean-03.mp4   the overhead glide - shark passing directly above camera

NOTE ON 03: the brief asked for a manta ray. There is no manta ray anywhere in
either dive clip - every animal in the footage is a bull shark. The overhead
glide is the closest thing to the awe beat a manta was meant to carry, and it
is genuinely the most beautiful two seconds in the folder.

02 comes from the landscape SportDiver clip cropped to 9:16 full frame, so it
is upscaled ~1.8x. Underwater detail is low enough that it holds, but it is
softer than 01 and 03, which are native portrait.
