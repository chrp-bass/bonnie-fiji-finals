## SHARK DIVE REEL (24s)

Twelve feet of bull shark, 80 feet down, twenty minutes off the beach. No cage.

The largest gathering of bull sharks anywhere on earth — and it exists for a reason most people never hear. In 2004 Galoa village, who traditionally own that reef, gave up their right to fish it. Every diver since pays a levy that funds their school. Fiji made it the country's first National Marine Park in 2014.

The sharks aren't luck. They're a deal.

Which is why the operator is the whole decision. I didn't book a shark dive. I booked Su — 1,500 dives, and he kept four first-timers calm at 80 feet.

## BULA BID CAROUSEL

Free one, because it costs me nothing and it might get you a flat bed to Nadi.

Fiji Airways runs an upgrade auction called Bula Bid. If your economy fare is eligible, they email you seven days before departure and invite you to name your own price for a lay-flat business seat.

The rules people don't know:
→ You can change or cancel your bid until **24 hours** before departure
→ You're told **18–24 hours** out
→ If you lose, you are **not charged a cent**

And no — not every bid wins. Ours did, at $1,200 for a lay-flat seat on an 18-hour flight.

## EDUCATIONAL CAPTION — THE FIREWALKERS OF BEQA

Here's something most travellers don't know about Fiji.

Firewalking isn't a Fijian thing. It's a **Beqa** thing — one island, one people, one bloodline.

The Sawau of Beqa call it *vilavilairevo*, which translates to "jumping into the earth oven." The story goes back about five hundred years to a man named Tuinaiviqalita, who went digging for an eel to gift a village elder and pulled up a *veli* — a spirit god — instead. The veli asked to be let go. In exchange it gave him, and everyone descended from him, power over fire.

It has never left those villages. Dakuibeqa. Dakuni. Soliyaga. Naceva. Naseuseu. Rukua. You cannot learn it. You are born into it or you are not.

I put my clients on an island in the Beqa Lagoon. The bartender's name is David. Halfway through the second week they found out his family are firewalkers — not performers hired for a Thursday show on Denarau, where a cultural night costs two hundred dollars. His actual family. Going back five centuries.

That conversation isn't on any itinerary. It isn't bookable. It happened because they were standing on the right island, talking to the right person, with enough nights to get past small talk.

That's what the fourteen days were for. That's what the fee is for.
