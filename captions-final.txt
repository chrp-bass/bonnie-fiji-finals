There is no field on a booking form for "it's her 50th."

They arrived at Nanuku after twenty-two hours of travel to a bed dressed with frangipani and a hand-painted sign that said BULA WELCOME HOME. Nobody asked them anything at check-in. They didn't have to — the property had known for weeks.

That's the whole difference between a booking and a brief. A search engine can rank ten thousand hotels by price. It cannot ring ahead, because it isn't talking to anyone.

I am. That's most of what the fee is actually for.

She cried, apparently. Then she ate the cake.

📍 Nanuku, Pacific Harbour, Fiji

You don't drive to Sabata Village. You go eleven miles up the Navua River by longboat.

Then you present kava to the chief and ask permission to come ashore.

It's called sevusevu. Knees and shoulders covered, hats off, and you drink the cup in one go.

Worth knowing before you turn up anywhere in Fiji.

📍 Navua River, Fiji
#Fiji #TravelTips
