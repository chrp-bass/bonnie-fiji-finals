You don't drive to Sabata Village. You go eleven miles up the Navua River by longboat.

Then you present kava to the chief and ask permission to come ashore.

It's called sevusevu. Knees and shoulders covered, hats off, and you drink the cup in one go.

Worth knowing before you turn up anywhere in Fiji.

📍 Navua River, Fiji
