## The story in one line

Denarau is 15 minutes from the Nadi airport — that's the entire reason it exists.
Bonnie sent them **55 miles the other way**, then split the fortnight in two:
**culture first** (Nanuku, 34 villas on 500 acres, a village that had to grant permission),
**island second** (Royal Davui, 16 villas, no day visitors).

The moat: she knows the owners of the island, she's friends with the pro surfer who
found the breaks, and she's friends with **Su**, who has 1,500 dives.
She didn't search. She called.

## Before you post

- **The reels have no audio.** That's the first pass in CapCut. Soft instrumental, no
  lyrics, ~85–95 BPM for the master; keep the original underwater sound on the shark
  reel; rotor sound then silence on the micro-reel. Turn auto-captions OFF — the text
  is already burned in.
- **Story stickers are drawn in as a guide.** Drop the real Instagram poll/slider/quiz
  sticker on top before posting, or taps won't register.
- **Bula Bid is a bid, not a guarantee.** The copy says so on purpose — "not every bid
  wins, ours did." Keep that framing.
- **The client-POV share card is for the clients.** Send it to them; let them post it.
